# Week 4 – Stakeholder Register

## Stakeholder Register

| Stakeholder | Affiliation | Interest | Influence | Strategy | Key Concern | Communication |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chief Medical Officer | RCHN, Internal | High | High | Manage Closely | Patient care and clinic workflow | Bi-weekly meeting |
| RCHN Board of Directors | RCHN, Internal | Low | High | Keep Satisfied | Budget and project milestones | Monthly executive summary |
| Clinic Intake Coordinators | RCHN, Internal | High | Low | Keep Informed | Reliable intake and scheduling workflow | Bi-weekly meeting |
| Healthcare Privacy / Regulatory Representatives | Regulatory Body, External | Low | High | Keep Satisfied | Privacy and compliance requirements | As-needed updates |
| RCHN IT Support and Infrastructure Team | RCHN, Internal | High | Low | Keep Informed | Technical readiness and maintenance | Bi-weekly meeting |
| Community Partner Organizations | Community / Public, External | Low | Low | Monitor | Changes to referrals and downstream services | As-needed updates |

## Highest Priority Stakeholder

The Chief Medical Officer requires the most deliberate engagement because they have high interest and high influence. They are concerned about patient care and clinic operations and can approve major clinical workflow changes. If their concerns are overlooked, NovaTech could make decisions that later need to be changed. Keeping them involved in important decisions should help the project stay on track.

## Risk of Omission

One stakeholder group I considered but did not include was patients. Patients are affected by the intake and scheduling system, but at this stage I focused the register on stakeholders directly connected to project decisions, support, and oversight. I would add patients later if the project begins collecting direct patient feedback or if the system changes have a larger effect on their experience.

## Week 4 Takeaway

Stakeholder analysis helped me understand that not everyone connected to a project has the same level of interest or influence. The stakeholder register helps NovaTech decide who needs to be closely involved, who should be kept informed, and who only needs to be monitored. The register should also be updated as the project changes.
